Safety

How Arch keeps people safe, and what you can do.

If you are in danger

Contact local emergency services first. Then block and report the person in Arch.

Block

Blocking is silent and complete. The person leaves your matches for good, can't see your profile, and is never told.

Report

Report anyone from their profile or your conversation. A person reads every report, and you don't have to block someone to report them. They're never told who reported them.

Real accounts

Every account is tied to a phone. Someone who is removed can't simply sign up again, which makes throwaway accounts hard to make. It doesn't prove anyone is who they say they are, so trust what people do over what they say.

Meeting for the first time

  • Meet somewhere public, like a café or a restaurant.
  • Tell a friend where you're going, who you're meeting, and when you'll be back.
  • Get yourself there and home. Don't rely on the other person for a ride.
  • Keep your phone charged.
  • Trust your gut. It's always okay to leave.

Something else

Write to support@archdating.com. A person reads every message.
