Arch

Five people, held. A dating app with no swiping and no likes: each morning Arch introduces you to a few people, you're in their roster as they're in yours, and writing to someone is the whole mechanism.

Help

Contact

Write to support@archdating.com. A person reads every message. Say which phone you use and what happened, but don't send your password or anything from your Apple account.

Someone made you feel unsafe

Block and report them from their profile or your conversation. They're never told who reported them. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services first.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. It happens straight away. The Privacy Policy says exactly what is removed and the little that is kept.

Cancelling Arch Premium

Premium is managed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Arch. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Getting your data

In the app: Settings → Download your data.
