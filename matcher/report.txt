6. The control: is the starving cohort the cooldown's fault?
==============================================================================
  "Never show the same person twice" is a guarantee that runs out of people.
  Nights is 7, so a cooldown of 7 means nobody repeats all week.

  cooldown   pairs/night   empty night 7   persistent zero   met (median)
  none               795            2.6%              2.6%              7
  3                  668            5.5%              5.0%             12
  7                  626           13.0%              8.6%             16
  never              626           13.0%              8.6%             16

  The trade is real and it is the product decision, not a bug: the cooldown
  is what stops the same faces recurring, and it is also what strands the
  people whose candidate list is short. Compare the last two columns.

7. The control: how much of this is just a small city?
==============================================================================
  Candidate sets scale with the population. A cohort that starves at n=400
  and not at n=2400 is a launch problem, not a design problem.

     n     median candidates   p10    empty night 7   persistent zero
    300                  21     2           21.5%             14.0%
    600                  45     6           12.6%              7.9%
   1200                  91    13            7.2%              5.2%
   2400                 180    26            4.2%              2.6%

  Counts would have risen down this column and said the opposite. As a share
  of the city, a bigger pool is the single most effective fix available.
